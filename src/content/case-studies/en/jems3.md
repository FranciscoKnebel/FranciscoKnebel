## Context

JEMS 3, the Journal and Event Management System, supports the management of academic conferences. Submissions, peer reviews and notifications are part of the work handled by the platform.

## My contribution

JEMS 3 was a substantial part of my software engineering work during the UFRGS research and development period listed in my experience, from 2017 to 2021. That work was part of the project “Novo Método para Avaliação de Contestações de Projetos da Lei de Informática”.

## Engineering perspective

A conference workflow connects several stages of work. A useful review of a system in this domain follows a submission through review and communication, checking what each participant needs to understand at each step.

For me, this connects software engineering with research operations: the product must make the process usable for people working toward academic deadlines.

## Scope

This is a concise account of my involvement. It does not attribute later releases or current operational results to my work. The contribution described here is software development within the research project, rather than ownership of the complete platform.
